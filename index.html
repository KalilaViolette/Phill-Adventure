It's late, the house is asleep, and Phill can't stop thinking about the giant sundae hidden deep in the Ice Cream Cave. Help him waddle from his bedroom, through the house, all the way there. Jump over whatever's in his way, slide under anything flying at him, and watch out: the farther he goes, the faster it gets. If he trips, you can pick up right where he fell as many times as you like, but your score stops counting at the first trip. Start over to go for a high score.

Reset progress?

This clears your high score and hides the Start in spots you've found, so Phill starts fresh from his bedroom.
